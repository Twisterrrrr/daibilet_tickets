# Design System for Public Landing Parity

## System Scope Clarification

This design system applies **primarily to PUBLIC LANDING UI**.

Admin and catalog interfaces follow a different, stricter UI system defined in `.cursor/rules/ui-system.mdc`.

**Landing pages are allowed to:**

- be more spacious
- be more editorial
- use richer composition
- deviate from strict component reuse where narrative requires it

**Admin/catalog pages are NOT allowed** to use that freedom — they stay structured and dense.

See also: [ui-scope.md](ui-scope.md)

---

## 1. Core principle

Consistency with the reference landing family is more important than generic site uniformity.

Public landing pages may be richer, more spacious, and more editorial than ordinary catalog pages.

---

## 2. Spacing

Use generous spacing.

Prefer a rhythm based on:

- 8
- 12
- 16
- 24
- 32
- 48
- 64
- 80

Do not compress landing pages into dense catalog spacing.

---

## 3. Typography

Landing pages must keep clear hierarchy:

- Hero headline: dominant
- Section headings: strong and readable
- Supporting text: calm and editorial
- Metadata / utility text: subordinate

Avoid flattening all text into uniform sizes.

---

## 4. Containers

Landing content should feel intentionally composed.

Do not wrap the entire page in a generic cramped container if that weakens the hero or section rhythm.

---

## 5. CTA hierarchy

Keep a clear CTA strategy:

- one dominant primary CTA per major block
- secondary actions remain visually subordinate
- avoid button overload
- do not turn all links into equal-weight controls

---

## 6. Cards and sections

Cards may exist, but the page must not become "just a grid of cards".

Editorial sections, explanatory sections, thematic blocks, and narrative transitions should remain intact.

---

## 7. Visual tone

**Desired tone:**

- confident
- editorial
- promotional
- travel / leisure / event-oriented
- premium but accessible

**Avoid:**

- admin-like density
- enterprise dashboard feeling
- catalog-only minimalism
- over-decoration unrelated to the reference family

---

## 8. Shell elements

Daibilet header/footer must visually frame the experience without overpowering the landing body.

Header/footer are infrastructure.

The landing remains the star.

---

## 9. Responsiveness

Responsive adaptation must preserve:

- hero priority
- CTA visibility
- section sequence
- content legibility

Do not solve mobile by deleting the landing character.
